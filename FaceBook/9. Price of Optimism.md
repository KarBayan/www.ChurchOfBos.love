### (9) Price of Optimism 9/15/2026

[I am moving to Colombia and giving away my collection of paintings, each with its own story.]

A small bluish painting depicts a mother's love for a child. The price of this love can easily be lifelong employment and a mindless, debilitating job.

But what is the price of optimism?

I have a friend, Serg Graff. He was a lawyer back in Moscow. He came to the U.S. with his wife and 2 kids. Out of necessity, he took a job as an Uber driver while contemplating passing the bar exam and restarting his career in his 40s. During one of his trips, he was having a conversation with a client and complained about low pay and long hours. The gentleman suggested, “Try painting.” Serg responded, “I never painted, and what would I paint?” At that moment, the gentleman gave him a $10 bill as a tip and told him, “Paint the president from the banknote. Goodbye,” and left.

Serg gave it a try. The painting came out childish, colorful, and of dubious artistic quality. Nevertheless, he put it on eBay. To his great surprise, someone bought it for about $100. Serg made more paintings in his natural primitivist style and eventually became a somewhat popular artist, selling them for around $500 per piece.

Once, he showed me his painting of a dragon and a birdie. He cheerfully declared that he was going to sell it for $750. Really?!? He could have just as easily hoped to sell his shit in a can as an artistic installation. I was a little bit depressed at the time because of an ongoing divorce, but I loved his optimism and paid the full price for it on the spot. (For your information, Piero Manzoni canned his own shit as art in 1961, and in 2016 one can sold for €275,000.)

PS: The painting of a dragon and a birdie has an interesting history. Serg's wife bought a portrait of a mother with a child at a thrift shop. She saw it as an icon and asked Serg to paint her vision. It came out beautifully in the Russian Orthodox style. She is also Serg's manager, responsible for sales. After some thought, she asked Serg to upgrade the Christian icon to a dragon theme, since dragons are trendier in the current art market. Serg skillfully completed the upgrade.
