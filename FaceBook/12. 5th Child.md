### (12) 5th Child 9/17/2026

Once upon a time, I met a charming professor of mathematics. I liked her and proposed marriage to her. Of course, she asked for some time to think. What else would you expect from a professor of mathematics?

One of the dilemmas she struggled with was that (1) she believed children should be made in marriage, and (2) she did not want more kids because she had already had 2 from her first marriage.

After some intense thinking, she found a solution. She gave me a pot with a *Ginkgo biloba* seed and told me to grow it as our child. Shortly thereafter, she declined my marriage proposal.

What was she thinking? We followed my rule and avoided sex before marriage. Nevertheless, she conceived a child/seed. She left me with a child, expecting me to bring them up, and then dumped me. I thought only a man could do such a thing to a woman!

As a faithful father, I cared for my baby. For the 1st year, I grew it as an infant in a pot inside the house. Then I planted it outside. It survived the winter and then almost died during the summer. It lost all its leaves one day because of intense heat and sun. It is doing fine now, growing strong.

But I am leaving for Colombia, and the place where we are living will be sold. So I made the decision to put my 5th child up for adoption. And I found a lady who is willing to accept it.

My beloved math professor was like a cuckoo bird that leaves its eggs/seeds for others to bring up. Intriguingly, I once held a cuckoo bird in my own hands, and I have photos to prove it.

By the way, the *Ginkgo biloba* tree is an intriguing plant to research. There are documented occurrences in which some branches of a male tree turn female, a.k.a. localized sex conversion. The evolutionary reasoning is that “if females are lacking, then I reproduce by myself.”
