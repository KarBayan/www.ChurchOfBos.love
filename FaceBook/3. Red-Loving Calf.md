### (3) Red-Loving Calf 8/24/2026

My financial situation in Colombia is improving. Today, after buying a carrot for my favorite calf, I am left with 2,700 pesos (0.88 U.S. dollars). I also made friends at Mormon Church, and they bought me yogurt today.

Here, I introduce myself as Russian-Armenian, born and raised in the Soviet Union. I wear some of my red lifeguard shirts and call myself el [ruso camarada] rojo, a.k.a. the Red [Russian comrade]. It helps to avoid association with the stereotypical American gringo. Moreover, I started speaking Russian-to-Spanish through ChatGPT.

Of course, I would make friends with a red-loving calf. Check out the video—how tender it is with my neighbor's red car. It even fights bigger bulls for it. Of course, carrots are his favorite food because they are red. We made friends. I feed it a carrot every morning, and I even invited it over to my home to introduce it to my stepdaughter.

It would be a mistake to say that I live in rural Colombia. I am in Barbosa, a small town about the size of Natick, Massachusetts. Barbosa is locked in a mountain valley and has about the same number of people as Natick, living at about six times the density in the center of the 1–5-story town. A Russian comparison would be Krasnoarmeysk (Красноармейск)—literally "Red Army Town," or "Ciudad del Ejército Rojo" in Spanish—a small Soviet-era industrial and defense-research town outside Moscow with about 26,600 residents. Everything is within a 15–30-minute walk. In a 2-hour drive, there is Tunja, about the size of Boston, and in 2 more hours, one will reach Bogota, which is like New York City.

I am in a never-ending summer. Temperatures are constant throughout the year. It is like July in Moscow or August in Boston. In front of my house is a green area with two springs, which naturally attract the bulls and calves. (I have yet to see a female cow.) Animals come in the morning on their own, eat, sleep, and then leave in the evening.

My favorite calf is the most curious one. It is so intriguing to play with it in the mornings. I'm planning to enjoy life with it until it is slaughtered, I buy it out, or else.:)
