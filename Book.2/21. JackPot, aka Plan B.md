PS: It is very interesting how one's wishes can come true. After my 1st divorce, I was looking for a new home. I had two offers: a room at my father's apartment or a room at my friend's house. I chose both. Several days per week I would stay at my father's place, and the rest of the time I would be with my friend.

This arrangement was somewhat inconvenient for me, since I was doing a lot of driving between 2 of my homes. So I suggested that my father rent a room at my friend's house. The alternative arrangement would have been harder, since my father was missing an extra room for my friend. My father forcefully declined and said, "I will live with you only over my dead body." And his wish came true.

After he died, I cremated him and brought him to my room in a stylish black box. For the next two years, he was living with me at my friend's house. I changed his address to mine, received his letters, parked his car next to mine, etc. We also talked regularly. He was a good listener and helped me a lot.

Before the death [of his body], I asked him, "What can I do for you?" He replied, "Do whatever you want." I always wanted to be happy. So, I became happy. It took some effort and ingenuity, but where there is a will, there is a way.

My father also wished to live somewhere warm after retirement. So my brother picked him up, and he lived with my brother for a year in Qatar, a very hot place. My father also wished to return to Russia, so my brother's wife took him to Moscow, where he is now living with his grandkids.

Thus, judging by the positive experience of my father after the death [of his body], I am hopeful that my wishes will also come true and I will become the Jackpot and Orli will turn out to be the Optimism.
