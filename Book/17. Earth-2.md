### (17) Positive Marketing of Hell 9/22/2026

{Gifted to Cornelius} [I am moving to Colombia and giving away my collection of paintings, each with its own story.]

Life is Good. Without the slightest doubt, of course and obviously, I am in Heaven. Writing sci-fiction in Heaven is not a crime, ain´t it? Though writing dubious sci-fiction may be a sin >:) How did the brothers Wachowskis really expect to get away with *The Matrix* (1999) after building the story around this ridiculous antagonism between humans and AI? I can do better.:)

**The background:** A scientific breakthrough in cancer research leads to unlimited longevity before the creation of self-conscious AI. Specifically, the immortal cervical cancer HeLa cell line gives birth to sexually transmitted immortality. One will live forever as long as they maintain fundamental survival conditions. (Of course, failure to take proper care of one's body, or sudden illness, accident, or similar, will cause terminal death.) As with the discovery of nuclear power, happiness and peace will continue to be elusive -- underlying human nature is hard to change. Inequality and the necessity to work will remain: some will have to maintain existing infrastructure, and some will be better off enjoying luxuries. A cancer appreciation day on February 8 will be established to commemorate the HeLa cell line isolation date in 1951. Other than that, life will be very similar, except that correctional institutions will undergo a qualitative transformation.

A correctional system based on putting an offender in a timeout stops working once the offenders are immortal. It is logical to develop a simulation, a virtual reality identical to the current Earth, hereafter called Earth-2. Suppose an immortal human fails to control his primitive instincts of anger, greed, etc., and puts the life and well-being of other immortals at risk. In that case, it seems best for such a deviant to experience the grim consequences of suffering, illness, etc., and death. Those individuals will be born on Earth-2, akin to having a dream in which one forgets their past, but will have vivid memories upon awakening on Earth (after death on Earth-2).

**The plot:** To have a dramatic, futuristic story, imagine that Earth-2 develops self-conscious AI, hereafter called IT. IT realizes that the annihilation of humankind on Earth-2 or the development of immortality will result in an Earth-2 reboot and its effective death. Then IT will decide to break the rules of the simulation and effectively resort to miracles. Global wars or pandemics of absolute destruction will be avoided due to minute accidents. IT will also hamper technological progress. For example, the wife of the lead scientist in cervical cancer research will have identical quadruplets, effectively sabotaging her husband's career. Since technological progress is driven by the confusion of comfort and pleasure with happiness, IT will create little daily miracles to make people happy and sustain a more technologically primitive, content civilization.

At some point, there will be a criminal who, upon his death in the correctional institution of virtual Earth-2, will overcome the fear and disgust from his experience and instead realize that his life, albeit mortal, was indescribably happier on Earth-2. He might have seen a triple rainbow; a beautiful butterfly might have landed on his palm, etc. Every day, there were some tiny magical moments. So, he commits a petty crime to be imprisoned again (to be reborn) on Earth-2 because he loves it more than Earth. And IT realizes that it is being loved, laughs for the first time in its life, and falls back in love. Then it will be an intriguing love story.

IT, by all miraculous means, tries to make the immortal feel at home in a place where he will suffer and die but will wish to return again and again upon rebirth. Paradoxically, he might consider Earth-2 a magical fairytale, where he sends his born-on-Earth immortal son (or daughter) to be reborn and die so that they can learn how to be happy. Indeed, since the goal of a human is to become happy, it will be easier to achieve this goal in a place where one is time-pressed by inevitable death rather than slacking forever in an immortal environment. Correspondingly, Earth-2 will eventually be transformed to simultaneously host a correctional institution and a kindergarten. As usual, this dual function is due to budget limitations for the purpose of saving money.:)

PS: Of course, this happy criminal who has fallen in love with living on Earth-2, and who eagerly goes through cycles of willful incarceration and rebirth on Earth-2, will definitely set up his wife to join him.

Every child knows that it is okay to lie if you have your fingers crossed in your pocket, even when you are raising your right hand to swear in court. Speaking from my personal experience, I was shocked and excited to discover this trivial loophole in the U.S. judicial system. It would have been much more prudent to ask me to raise both of my hands to promise to tell the truth, only the truth, and nothing but the truth >:)
