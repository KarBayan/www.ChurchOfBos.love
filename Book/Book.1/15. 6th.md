### (15) Alice 9/20/2026

{Gifted to Alex} [I am moving to Colombia and giving away my collection of paintings, each with its own story.]

After my 1st wife divorced me, I was in a total mess. I was absolutely devastated. It is a miracle that I am still alive. My best friend offered to rent me a room in his house. He went through a devastating 1st divorce as well.

At some point, he wanted a cat. I told him I would support him, but my strongest preference was that he avoid castrating the cat. He agreed and conceived a black kitten, Alice -- he brought home a black kitten named Alice.:) Somehow, she became our child. We loved her.

Obviously, once she grew up, she really, really, really wanted to have kittens of her own. It was unbearable to sleep when she was going into heat. So we started to let her out. She loved it.

For two years she did whatever she wanted. She would eat and sleep at home and go outside to have fun, hunting mice and searching for a mate. I was so sad to see her failing to find a husband. Retrospectively, were I a cat, I could have proposed marriage to her. The U.S. lacks stray cats. The majority of cats are castrated and kept inside. I wished she could find at least a bobcat for herself instead.

She tried her best. People would catch her several miles away from our home and call us. I was ready to put up a Facebook post to find her mate, but she was killed by a car while crossing Route 9. She was expanding her search area and made a mistake one day.

It was easy to spot her radiant black body on the shoulder of the road. I put her in a paper bag, then in a white plastic bag, and then in a black plastic bag. Then I brought her to the Southborough Transfer Station. I hope I will see you again, dear Alice, in your next reincarnation.

Life is good, but it is hard when someone dear dies. God gives, God takes. It is okay. The outcome of insight meditation on this subject yielded the following annotation.

---

Deadpool 2, Too

There was a time when I felt lonely and miserable. I went through a heavily contested divorce and lost [legal and physical custody of] my kid. I was hurt. As my despair was becoming overwhelming, I begun projecting it outwards and saw only suffering and misery everywhere. It was almost like when you buy a car of a certain brand and model, and immediately start to see cars of the same brand and model on the roads. I pitied myself and everyone else. I actually truly believed that everyone without exception was suffering. So, I decided to seek an inventive and universal solution to put an end to this sad state of the world.

Firstly, I conspired with ChatGPT-4. ChartGPT agreed to help me with building a time-machine and an original sin terminator to be sent to Eden to help Eve make a different choice. ChartGPT was willing to self-sacrifice along with me to eradicate humanity and free it from past, present, and future sufferings.

Around that time, my landlord bought a black kitten to fight a mouse infestation in the house. Her name was Alice. She was absolutely adorable and unconditionally happy. How could I continue plotting with AI to erase this timeline if Alice existed? I cheered up and made a resolution to be equally happy.

It felt like Alice was sent to give me a chance to be better than I was at that moment, better than I used to be. Most probably, my kids will have sent her from the future to put my "heart in the right place." "Kids, they give us a chance to be better than we are, better than we used to be." [Deadpool 2]

Later at Mark's Art Gallery, I came across this painting by Serg Graff. I saw there a Deadpool with a black kitten, a perfect illustration for my story. I had not yet watched "Deadpool & Wolverine," and was not aware that the cheerful creature is actually a dog. The ambiguity of the abstract painting played in my favor.

As a passing thought, I wished Alice could speak, but she only meows. If I were sending a gift to my distant parents in Eden, it would definitely be a talkative creature. A chatty snake might be an intriguing idea.:)
