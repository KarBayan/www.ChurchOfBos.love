One woman told me that to be successful with a woman, it is better that I think like a woman. Okay. So, thinking like a woman, I concluded that sex would be after marriage, and consequently it would be best if any dating happened outside, for example, in a natural setting.

Next to my home was a secluded conservation area, a very large field with occasional trees. The area was crisscrossed by trails, some of which were actually old, narrow paved roads. So one warm day, during the Halloween season, I took the lady for a walk in this park.

Of course, everyone is busy working, so we started late in the afternoon. The sun set quickly, but it was a full moon night, and it was perfectly bright enough to continue walking. She said that she felt safe, but she confessed that she really, really liked watching thrillers, and the setting fit perfectly into multiple stereotypical horror scenarios. So whenever she was triggered by a sudden gust of wind, a cloud covering the moon, an owl making a noise, deer appearing at the edge of the forest, the crack of a branch underfoot, etc., she would panic and run. After each sprint, she would apologize. We would laugh and continue walking. We sprinted numerous times. It was the sweatiest date I had ever had.

Be careful with what you watch, listen to, and read.:) The power of unintentional stereotypes is not to be underestimated, ain’t it?
