### (1) Loving Colombia 8/22/2026

I’m in Colombia for a little over a month. I started teaching English, and I am in the active process of being hired. A decision was made—I am staying in Colombia for at least the next 5 years.

Tentative plans are:

1. Support any worthy candidates running for mayor in local towns. The ultimate goal is to build a mayors’ political party and have candidates on the ballot for Congress and the presidency by 2031. “Aspirantes a la Alcaldía.”

Alcalde [ahl-KAHL-deh] translates as “mayor,” and alcaldía [ahl-kahl-DEE-ah] as “the mayor’s office” or “municipal administration.” The first time I asked a local—through ChatGPT—for directions to the town hall, I was confused when he pointed to a big white building and told me that it was the headquarters of their al-Qaeda terrorist organization. Intriguingly, alcaldía and al-Qaeda sound very similar.

2. Continue active development of [www.ChurchOfBos.love](http://www.ChurchOfBos.love) and execution of its projects.

3. Help my wife with her fashion business.

4. Optional: learn Spanish.

I’m loving my utterly surreal and intriguing life in Colombia. For the last 3 days, I was locked out of my finances and was living off cash only. Luckily, I found 50 pesos (1.6 U.S. cents), and we were able to buy cat food in the evening.:)
