**The Pledge:** Dear God, on this Christmas Eve, I have a complaint about the choices you and Jesus made. I believe that dying on a cross is both gross and distressing.

Personally, I much prefer the story of Moses leading people through the desert to build their physical and spiritual faith. That story is purely uplifting. I’d gladly walk with Moses anytime, provided that, along with the daily manna, there’s some variety in the menu. A bit of carrots and cabbage would be much appreciated.:)

Anyway, ChatGPT and I wrote you and Jesus an alternative scenario—one that replaces dying on the cross with loving a woman. This story celebrates the boundless possibilities that love can offer.

**The Scenario:** In Judea lived a woman named Margo who fell deeply in love with Jesus. She loved the man behind the miracles: his laughter, his kindness, his tired feet after a long journey, his delight in children, and the warmth with which he shared bread among friends. Jesus loves all and All, and he loved her too.

One evening beneath an olive tree in Jerusalem, Margo took his hands. “Stay,” she said. “Let your love for humanity become a life among humanity. Teach us how to love through every season: as a teacher, a husband, a father, a friend, and one day an old man.”

Jesus carried her words into prayer. Heaven answered. A messenger appeared and said, “Every destiny contains choices. Love opens another road. Walk it fully, and let your life become the teaching.” Jesus smiled.:)

The next morning he returned to Margo. “I have good news. I really like you. Will you marry me?” Of course, she married him, and they traveled together through Judea and beyond. Jesus continued healing, teaching, forgiving, challenging the powerful, welcoming strangers, and sitting at tables with people whom respectable society preferred to avoid. Margo walked beside him, adding her own wisdom, tenderness, humor, and occasional correction. They had children.

And humanity received a different Gospel: Love God. Love one another. Live fully. And let Love have time to finish its work.
