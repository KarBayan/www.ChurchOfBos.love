### (39) Adopting mother 11/1/2026 #### Book.2 ##### Book

I used to live by just 2 rules: (1) do no harm and (2) believe in magic. What harm would there be in talking at the gym to a charming lady 26 years older than me? And would it be safe to discuss our families with her?

-- My mother died about 20 years ago. I miss her, and I wish I still had a mother.  
-- Sorry. Some things cannot return. I always wanted to have a 2nd son, but it never happened; it is too late for me now.  
-- Do you believe in magic?  
-- Yes.  
-- Would you agree if I adopted you? I will “reincarnate” a mother for myself, and you will get a 2nd son.  
-- Yes.

We hugged and sealed our mutual adoption.

With my adopted mother, Mariya, came some responsibilities -- I take care of her [she takes care of me]. But there are also benefits. My family expanded. In addition to my living father, I gained a stepdad. [By the way, he shares my birthday, the 6th of December.] I also got a brother and 2 sisters. My children got a grandmother.

Mothers pass Judaism to their children. I had a Jewish mother now. So, I’m a Jew now, ain’t I? Though some told me that without circumcision my conversion is incomplete, I personally feel more complete avoiding circumcision.:) It is so intriguing to be a Russian-Armenian-Jew with a blood composition of 75% Russian, 25% Armenian, and 0% Jew. Though the best thing about being a Jew is having Shabbat on Saturdays and a valid excuse to have a spiritual day off.

Finally, I decided to change my name, shortening it from Karen Gurgenovich Balabanyan to Kar Bayan. Changing my name through the probate and family court took about 9 months. It was complicated.

Going through the probate and family court again to formalize the adoption felt boring. So, I decided to do something different and intriguing. I invented a church adoption! Under the name Karen, I was an ordained minister of Universal Life Church Monastery ([themonastery.org](https://www.themonastery.org/)). I had my license and 2 beautiful marriage certificates. With 2 simple corrections, Karen transformed those marriage certificates into adoption certificates, one for me another for my mother, and united Kar and Mariya in a mother-son relationship on October 22, 2022.

I loved the simplicity of church procedures. Doesn’t God love children and parents, dudes and gals, etc. and etc., and wish them a happy life without bureaucratic complications, as well as without pricey and time-consuming rituals? I am sure it would be right for [www.ChurchOfBos.love](http://www.ChurchOfBos.love) to serve the optimism of those seeking happy relationships by providing a registry for marriages, adoptions, friendships, mentorships, business partnerships, etc.
