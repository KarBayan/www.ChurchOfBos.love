### (33) Favorite blue 10/19/2026 #### Book.2 ##### Book

-- [she] I saw the most beautiful shade of blue the first time we kissed. It was such a strange experience, and I felt peace and security.  
-- [he] And what about your recurring nightmares about drowning?  
-- [she] The fear is gone. But why did you mention that?  
-- [he] Because mermaids are your favorite theme to doodle. And, as you know, a girl who drowns and is lost becomes a mermaid. That blue is the color of the sky you saw when I saved you and you opened your eyes.  
-- [she] You're joking, aren't you?  
-- [he] It is true. I did that in a dream before you were born, so that you could be born.  
-- [she] Did you travel into the past in your dream?  
-- [he] Indeed. In a dream, one's soul is free from the constraints of time and space set by one's body.  
-- [she] Who told you that?  
-- [he] My teacher. I had a dream about him. It went like this. Evening comes to a beach. He has spent the day with his grandchildren--teaching them how to swim, laughing with them as they "got it"--and now he is wrestling with his youngest grandson in the sand. Of course, he loses, and the child declares victory. He is tired and sits down in a beach chair; his heart is still pounding from all the physical activity, and his head is full of childish excitement. He starts to relax physically and calm down mentally. His breathing slows, and the children continue to laugh. His eyes are still open as he follows the fading sun setting toward the ocean's horizon. He feels and knows with certainty that there is nothing bad or good in the world; if he ever saw either, it was his misunderstanding. He will fall asleep peacefully, surrounded by love. He laughs because he is dying to be born again, maybe tomorrow with the sunrise.  
-- [she] And?  
-- [he] He said that was a true story.  
-- [she] And?  
-- [he] He said that there are ways to come back. He told me that I sneaked back out of dusk. The first thing I saw was the dark blue sky of the east.  
-- [she] Is that why you turn east, away from the sunset's red and orange sky, to contemplate the blue evening sky?  
-- [he] Yes.  
-- [she] Did he teach you how to bring back the dead?  
-- [he, laughing out loud] He told me to become a lifeguard and contemplate that subject further. That was 2 years ago. One night, I dreamed of saving a little girl, and then we met. We fell in love, we kissed, and you started to remember.  
-- [she] How did you die?  
-- [he] I fell off a golden horse and broke my neck.  
-- [she] There are no golden horses. You're fooling me, aren't you?  
-- [he] A carnival came to town. There were hot-air balloons, a Ferris wheel, and a merry-go-round. It was late in the evening; I was tired and sleepy. It was an accident. I fell off a golden horse.  
-- [she] Please kiss me again.  
-- [he] Will you marry me?
