Can I wish someone happiness without taking responsibility for making them happy? Conversely, can I wish myself happiness without making someone responsible for regulating me--for example, by requiring others to behave in certain ways and do certain things so that I can feel and perceive the world a certain way?

Do I really know how to wish, or do I simply want, want, want? Can I overcome my demands for a specific outcome and state my desires as a wish that is open to negotiation and cooperation?

And what is reasonable care?
