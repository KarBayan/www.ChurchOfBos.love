### (18) Life in Heaven, 18+ 9/23/2026

Life is Good. But I was missing being married. Yesterday, September 22nd, 2026, my wish came true.

Long story short, on September 5th, on the last day of my son's vacation in Colombia, we stopped at Didi's mall in Bogota to get me a data plan. I love all women, but I really liked Orli in customer service. So I gave her my phone number. Later that day, she texted me back and asked me to stop by again and pick up the SIM card.

The very same day, my son and I returned to the mall. Orli and I talked briefly because she was busy at work. It was the 2nd time I had seen her. My initial judgment was correct. (Trust yourself but retest.) She is the one to propose to.

The next morning at 4 a.m., my son and I left for the U.S. She wished to see the U.S., so I kept her in my heart and gave her a virtual tour of the U.S., starting by sending photos from Logan Airport in Boston. Shortly thereafter, I proposed to her. She accepted. She was enjoying her visit to the U.S., and I was enjoying our video conversations in Bogota on her way back from work.

On September 22nd, her 17-day visit to the U.S. was over. I arrived in Bogota, and we got married. She agreed to my brilliant idea to have the warmest and sweetest marriage, the pinky-promise one. I was doing this kind of stuff when I was a kid, and I loved it.

We entangled our pinky fingers, and she composed our vows: “My dear Kar/Orli, I promise—I promise to take care of you. I promise to be with you in sickness and in health, for richer or for poorer, until death do us part. Do you take me to be your Husband/Wife? Yes/Yes.”

LOL, do I believe in death? I believe in eternal life, so she has inadvertently got stuck with me forever.:) The best thing to do forever and ever is to make love. So I took off my coat and put it on her. She was feeling cold. Thank God, there are so many different ways to love. Then we walked outside holding hands. I love Orli >:)

However, the next morning, a grim realization struck me: she might believe in death. Dear God, how can one possibly believe in death in Heaven? Houston, we've got a problem >:)

My 1st wife chose a paper marriage. Divorce in a paper marriage is to file papers and get more papers. My 2nd wife chose a Facebook marriage. Divorce in a Facebook marriage is to change your status. My 1st and 2nd wives easily divorced me. But my 3rd wife, Orli, chose a pinky-promise marriage with the vow: “...until death do us part.”

Oh Lord, I better behave or else... Sorry, but just in case, because everyone makes mistakes, I will modify my current prayer of the heart to be: “Dear wife Orli, daughter of God, have mercy on me, a sinner.”
