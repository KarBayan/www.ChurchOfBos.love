### (20) Entangled (It's complicated) 9/26/2026

{Gifted to Thyself} [I am moving to Colombia and giving away my collection of paintings, each with its own story.]

Today is a very sad, warm, and sunny day. My 3rd wife Orli became a widow. I love her, and I am doing my best to support her in this difficult moment in her life.

Among other things, I am a Buddhist, and today I remembered that Buddha supposedly famously said, “Every morning we are born again.” Of course, it is obvious that every evening we die. Our pinky-promise marriage with my 3rd wife Orli was “...until death do us part.” Last night I fell asleep, and death parted us. My 3rd wife Orli is a widow now.

There is a chance that she will resurrect our marriage because yesterday, before my death, I made her a Facebook marriage proposal. If she accepts it, then we will be married again. I hope she loves me and really, really likes me. May I be married again.:)

Losing a spouse, or just any loved one, is very serious. Processing grief is important and will take some time. I will help her start the recovery process. For her husband's funeral, I bought her flowers. Later in the evening, we may have a quiet dinner and fondly remember all the good things that happened during her short pinky-promise marriage.

I sincerely wish her to leave the past in the past, look to the future with optimism, and marry as soon as possible. Surely I can help her find a new husband. In fact, I know a very good candidate. >÷)

PS: We had a wonderful evening. Orli pointed out that irrespective of my death, we can continue to be entangled: “both of us call each other husband and wife and keep relevant wallpapers on our phones.” With this understanding, I retracted my Facebook marriage proposal and set my relationship status to “It's complicated.”
