### Quiero Bien

***The practical work: projects, initiatives, plans, and collaborations.***

We are developing a global franchise [www.QuieroBien.com](http://www.QuieroBien.com). The 1st installment will be in Colombia [www.QuieroBien.com.co](http://www.QuieroBien.com.co). And the non-profit sibling of [www.QuieroBien.com](http://www.QuieroBien.com) is going to be [www.QuieroBien.org](http://www.QuieroBien.org).

The idea for QuieroBien.com/.org comes from the Spanish saying "te quiero bien." "Te quiero" translates as "I love you" or "I care about you." So "te quiero bien" can be interpreted as "I want you to be well." Thus QuieroBien.com/.org are the companies that **want good** and **love well**.
